# TENDERFORGE — PROPOSAL GENERATION & DRAFTING GUIDE

**Project:** TenderForge — From Tender to Trustworthy Proposal  
**Phase:** Phase 2  
**Date:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Overview & Zero Fabrication Mandate

The **Proposal Architect Service** (`backend/app/services/proposal_service.py`) automates the creation of high-scoring, persuasive technical and commercial proposals grounded strictly in verified corporate evidence.

### The Zero Fabrication Mandate
Most AI proposal generators suffer from hallucination, inventing client names, turnover statistics, or technical capabilities when data is missing.

In TenderForge:
- **No unsupported claim is ever invented.**
- If evidence is absent, the generator inserts an explicit action placeholder:
  `[ACTION REQUIRED: Supporting evidence missing for requirement <req_id> (<category>). Human confirmation or document upload required.]`
- Every factual claim generates a corresponding ProofLink citation footnote.

---

## 2. Standard 5-Chapter Proposal Structure

Proposals generated by TenderForge follow the standard procurement evaluation structure:

### Section 1.0: Executive Summary & Corporate Profile
- Introduces the bidding entity, legal registration numbers, and operational standing.
- Highlights audited financial turnover capacity backed by cited audit reports.

### Section 2.0: Technical Approach & Solution Architecture
- Directly addresses each technical specification clause extracted from the tender.
- Cites verified technical capabilities and infrastructure specifications.

### Section 3.0: Past Performance & Verifiable References
- Summarizes audited past performance records from the company evidence repository.
- Provides verifiable client completion details (contract numbers, values, completion years).

### Section 4.0: Quality Assurance & Certified Standards
- Details compliance with international standards (ISO 9001, ISO 27001, CMMI).
- Lists accredited registrar names and validity periods.

### Section 5.0: Compliance Matrix & Statutory Undertakings
- Embeds the evaluated compliance matrix directly into the proposal.
- Includes mandatory non-debarment and statutory declarations.

---

## 3. Microsoft Word (.docx) Generation

The service utilizes `python-docx` to generate publication-grade Word documents:
- **Typography:** Formatted headings, professional line spacing (1.15x), clean paragraph margins.
- **Color Palette:** Slate corporate palette (Slate-900 headers, Slate-500 subtitles).
- **ProofLink Footnotes:** Every claim is rendered as a stylized bullet citation with color-coded status badges (Green for Verified, Red for Contradicted, Amber for Pending).

---

## 4. Revision Tracking & Snapshots

Each draft generation increments the proposal version (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, etc.) and stores an immutable snapshot in `proposal_revisions`. This allows the team to inspect differences between versions, track modifications made by agents or humans, and revert changes when necessary.
