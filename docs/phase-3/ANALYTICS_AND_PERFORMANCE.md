# TenderForge Analytics & Operational Intelligence

## 1. Overview & Objectives

The **Analytics and Performance Intelligence Service** computes quantitative metrics evaluating:
- Pipeline Velocity & Volume
- Compliance Matrix Health
- Proposal Evidence Linkage Ratio
- Multi-Agent Collaboration Efficiency & Human Governance Override Ratios.

---

## 2. Metric Categories & Formulas

### 1. Pipeline Metrics
- **Total Tenders Processed**: Total opportunities managed in the workspace.
- **Active Pipeline Value**: Sum of estimated tender values in active flight.
- **Average Proposal Preparation Cycle**: Time from tender document ingestion to human executive approval.

### 2. Compliance Metrics
- **Compliance Pass Rate**:
  $$\text{Pass Rate} = \frac{\text{Count of Compliant Matrices}}{\text{Total Evaluated Matrices}} \times 100$$
- **Mandatory Requirements Density**: Proportion of requirements tagged as mandatory vs optional.

### 3. Proposal & ProofLink Metrics
- **ProofLink Verification Rate**:
  $$\text{Verification Rate} = \frac{\text{Verified Claim Lineages}}{\text{Total Claim Lineages}} \times 100$$
- **Average Bid Readiness Score**: Mean readiness score output by the independent `BidAuditor`.

### 4. Human-Agent Collaboration Metrics
- **Recommendation Distribution**: Breakdown of `BID`, `BID WITH CONDITIONS`, and `NO-BID RECOMMENDED`.
- **Human Decision Override Ratio**:
  $$\text{Override Ratio} = \frac{\text{Human Overrides}}{\text{Total Human Decisions}} \times 100$$
