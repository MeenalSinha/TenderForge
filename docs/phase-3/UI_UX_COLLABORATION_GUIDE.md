# TenderForge UI/UX Collaboration Guide

## 1. Overview & Experience Principles

TenderForge unifies human bid managers, subject matter experts, and 5 specialized AI agents into a synchronized workspace. The user experience is designed around transparency, evidence traceability, and human control.

## 3. Key Collaboration Patterns

1. **Sidecar Evidence Inspector**: Clicking any cited claim in the proposal opens a slide-over panel displaying the exact page and highlighted paragraph in the underlying PDF/DOCX evidence file.
2. **Side-by-Side Proposal Optimizer**: Shows original draft text vs AI-enhanced text side-by-side with diff highlights and predicted score increases.
3. **One-Click Human Sign-Off**: Generates a cryptographic SHA-256 signature hash confirming the executive's identity, role, and approval comments.
